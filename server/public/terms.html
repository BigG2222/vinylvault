What VinylVault does

VinylVault identifies vinyl records from photos, estimates their condition, checks the pressing and shows price suggestions from Discogs where available. It is free to use while it is new, with a daily scan limit.

Estimates, not guarantees

  • Identification, grades and pressing results are AI estimates from photos. They can be wrong.
  • A pressing result (for example “Likely Original” or “Possible Bootleg”) is not proof of authenticity. Don't accuse a seller on this result alone.
  • Prices are Discogs price suggestions at the time shown, not sales, valuations or appraisals. When there is no Discogs price the app says so. Check current prices on Discogs before you buy or sell.
  • Photos can't show how a record sounds. Play it before you rely on a grade.
