## Answer to the specific Discogs question

**Do any paid features currently use Discogs data in a way the terms would not allow? No, not today.** The app has no live paid features: `FREE_LAUNCH` is true, every user gets Premium for free, no store products or RevenueCat keys exist, and the paywall can't be reached from the normal UI.

**But two Premium-gated features are built on Discogs data:** "Value at every grade" (Discogs price suggestions) and CSV/PDF export (which contains Discogs prices). Turning `FREE_LAUNCH` off would immediately put Discogs Restricted Data behind a subscription. Your own reading of the terms says that needs Discogs' written permission. Keep `FREE_LAUNCH = true` until Discogs replies in writing.
