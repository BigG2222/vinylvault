Common questions

How do I get the best result? Photograph the front cover, back cover and centre label in even light, without flash. Adding the runout etching (the writing between the last groove and the label) and the barcode helps identify the exact pressing.

How accurate is it? Results are estimates from photos, not appraisals. Every result shows how confident it is, and you can pick a different pressing if the top match is wrong. Photos can't show how a record sounds, so play it before you buy or sell on a grade.

Where do the prices come from? From Discogs marketplace data where available. Data provided by Discogs. Discogs prices are refreshed once they are more than 6 hours old.

This application uses Discogs’ API but is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Discogs. ‘Discogs’ is a trademark of Zink Media, LLC.

Why does it say I've used my scans? VinylVault is free while it's new, with 10 scans a day. The count resets at midnight.

Where is my collection stored? Only on your phone. Use the export button in Collection to save a CSV or PDF copy.
