VinylVault privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

VinylVault is a mobile app that identifies, grades and values vinyl records from photos. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and who it is shared with. VinylVault has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Photos of your records

When you scan a record, the photos you take or choose (front cover, back cover, label, runout and barcode) are sent over an encrypted connection to the VinylVault server, which passes them to Anthropic's Claude AI service to identify the record and estimate its condition. The VinylVault server does not save your photos: they are used for that one analysis and then discarded. Anthropic processes them as our service provider under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to use them to train its models. Please photograph records, not people or personal documents.

Record details and prices

To show prices and links, the server looks up the identified record (artist, title, label, catalogue number, country, year, barcode) on Discogs. Nothing about you is sent to Discogs.

This application uses Discogs’ API but is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Discogs. ‘Discogs’ is a trademark of Zink Media, LLC.

Your collection

Your collection, scan history, notes, tags and the photos you keep are stored only on your phone. They are not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them. You can export your collection yourself as CSV or PDF.

Usage limits

To keep the free service running, the server counts scans per day. For this it uses a random ID the app creates when installed (it is not linked to your name, phone number, Google or Apple account, or advertising ID) and your internet (IP) address. These counts are kept in memory only and are cleared every day. The server's logs record the time and type of each request and any error, never your photos.

Camera and photo access

The app asks for camera access to photograph records and read barcodes, and opens photos from your library only when you choose them. Some text on the label is read on your phone using Google's on-device ML Kit, which does not send the image anywhere.

Reports and support

If you report an incorrect result or contact support, the app opens your email app. We receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to reply and improve results.

Children

VinylVault is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can stop all data sharing by not scanning, and delete everything stored by the app by uninstalling it. Because we hold no account or stored personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete; if you have questions, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
